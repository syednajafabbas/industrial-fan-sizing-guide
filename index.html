1. Understanding CFM & Volumetric Airflow

CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) measures how much air volume moves past a point every minute. For full unit background and definitions, see the Cubic foot per minute (CFM) article on Wikipedia.

When reading technical submittals, engineers also separate ACFM (Actual CFM) at site temperature and pressure from SCFM (Standard CFM) corrected to standard air density. In hot climates (45–50°C rooftops), thin air reduces mass flow, so always check motor ambient temperature ratings.

CFM = (Room Volume in ft³ × ACH) ÷ 60
1 CFM = 1.699 m³/h = 0.47195 L/s

2. Static Pressure & Why "Free-Air" Ratings Fail

Static pressure—measured in Pascals (Pa) or inches of water gauge (in. wg) where 1 in. wg ≈ 249 Pa—is the resistance created by ductwork, elbows, louvers, dampers, and filters. A fan's real operating output is its Duty Point: where the fan curve crosses your system resistance curve.

  • Free-discharge (25–75 Pa): Wall shutters or roof exhaust with no duct—axial fans work well.
  • Short/medium ductwork (100–450 Pa): Elbows, branches, and grilles—use centrifugal inline or centrifugal roof fans.
  • Kitchen hoods & filters (500+ Pa): Grease baffles and long risers—use heavy-duty upblast roof fans or centrifugal blowers.

3. Saudi 60Hz Power & Drive Considerations

Matching local power frequency is critical when ordering industrial ventilation equipment in Saudi Arabia.

60Hz vs. 50Hz Curves

A 4-pole motor spins at ~1,750 RPM on 60Hz vs. ~1,450 RPM on 50Hz. That 20% speed jump increases power draw by ~73% (cube law), risking motor overload if a 50Hz-only unit is misapplied.

220V 1Ph vs. 380V 3Ph

Light ceiling and inline units (like KDK and TAT) typically use 220–240V 1-phase 60Hz, while large roof fans and blowers use 380–400V 3-phase 60Hz.

Direct vs. Belt Drive

Direct-drive fans minimize rooftop belt maintenance. Belt-drive units keep motors out of hot or greasy airstreams and allow pulley adjustments during commissioning.

4. Common Sizing Questions

Why is free-air CFM misleading when selecting an exhaust fan?

Free-air CFM is measured at 0 Pa with no ducts, grilles, or filters attached. Real system resistance drops delivered airflow, so always verify CFM at your project's static pressure.

How do you convert CFM to m³/h and L/s?

Multiply CFM by 1.699 for m³/h, or by 0.47195 for L/s. Divide m³/h by 1.699 to convert back to CFM.

What should I send when requesting a fan quote in Saudi Arabia?

Share required CFM (or m³/h), static pressure (Pa or in. wg), mounting type, duct/opening dimensions, application (general air, kitchen grease, warehouse heat), voltage/phase (60Hz), and delivery city.
